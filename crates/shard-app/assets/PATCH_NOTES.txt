0.1.0 PREVIEW

Opening projects
- Opens projects saved by industry-standard texturing software: meshes, texture sets, the full layer stack with
  masks, fills, generators, filters and paint strokes.
- Shows each texture set as soon as it is composed; progress in the status bar.
- Recent projects on the start screen; recovery of unsaved work after a crash.

Texturing
- Layers, groups, masks and effects with blend modes and opacity, edited live.
- Paint and eraser with brush presets and alphas; the eyedropper picks from the mesh.
- Masks: invert, invert background, clear; mask preview.
- Drag over the eyes to show or hide many layers or texture sets in one stroke.
- Texture sets can be hidden or isolated; the brush only reaches what is shown.

Viewport
- Physically based shading with image-based lighting, bloom and a path-traced mode.
- Channel and mesh-map views, environment rotation and exposure.

Export
- Texture export per set with presets and padding past the UV islands.

Interface
- Windows that dock, tab, split and float; lock the layout in Settings.
- Assets shelf with thumbnails, filters and grid or list view.
- Pixel-font interface with a purple highlight; three animation levels.
- Industry-standard keyboard shortcuts.

Not yet
- Material authoring (the node graph) is laid out but not built.
- Mesh map baking.
